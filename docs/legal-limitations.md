# Legal limitations

WorldPrize is a technical demo, not legal advice.

## Important caveats

- Promotion laws vary by jurisdiction.
- Eligibility, age, regional restrictions, and tax treatment still matter.
- A free-entry path may still require formal legal review.
- World ID reduces Sybil-style abuse; it does not create legal compliance by itself.

## Demo disclaimer

Use this language in the UI:

> Demo only. No real prize is awarded. Promotion laws, eligibility, tax, age, regional restrictions, and no-purchase requirements still apply. World ID only helps reduce duplicate-human abuse on the free-entry path.
